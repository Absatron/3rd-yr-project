Research High Performance and High Throughput Computing (HPC/HTC)
Get help with accessing, costing and software requests on our HPC/HTC service

Our Research High Performance and High Throughput Computing (HPC/HTC) Service offers an on-campus, batch-based platform tailored to support computationally-intensive research across all disciplines. This service is fully integrated with our Research Data Storage (RDS) system, ensuring enhanced data management and accessibility.

You can request access to HPC/HTC services such as CSF, iCSF, Condor, and associated systems like SSH Gateways. Additionally, you may request access to software or N8 Bede resources, a high-performance computing facility that is part of the N8 Research Partnership.

Key Features:

Optimised for High Throughput Computing (HTC)
Ideal for short, small Central Processing Unit (CPU) jobs
A number of nodes support long-running jobs
A selection of Graphics Processing Units (GPUs) for specialized tasks

All researchers and students are eligible to request this service.

We aim to process your request within 5 working days.
